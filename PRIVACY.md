Keysmith Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 29, 2026
Keysmith – Custom Keyboard Shortcuts ("Keysmith") is a browser extension published by Fernlight Labs.
What we collect
Nothing. Keysmith does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history, page content, or usage analytics. It contains no tracking, no analytics and no remote code, and it makes no network requests of its own.
What stays on your device
Your shortcut settings (key combinations, chosen actions, site rules) are saved with Chrome's built-in extension storage. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own browsers under your Google account; Fernlight Labs never receives them.
Permissions and why they are needed
storage – save your shortcut settings.
tabs – perform the tab actions you assign (switch, create, close, pin, mute, move tabs) and check a page's address against your per-site rules. Addresses are only compared locally and never recorded or sent anywhere.
scripting – make shortcuts work immediately in tabs that were already open when Keysmith was installed or updated.
sessions – the "Reopen closed tab" action.
Access to websites – listen for your key presses on the sites where your shortcuts are allowed, and run page actions such as scrolling and video controls. Keysmith does not read, store or transmit page content.
bookmarks (optional, only if you create an "Open bookmark" shortcut) – list your bookmarks so you can pick one.
Exported files
If you export your settings, the file is saved wherever you choose on your computer. Keysmith does not upload it.
